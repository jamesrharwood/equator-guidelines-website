**Do not** use {{< meta acronym >}} if you are:

* writing an observational research article, use [STROBE](/reporting-guidelines/strobe) instead.
* writing a proposal or protocol for a trial, use [SPIRIT](/reporting-guidelines/spirit) instead.
* appraising the quality of a randomised trial, use an appraisal tool like the [CASP Randomised Controlled Trial Checklist](https://casp-uk.net/casp-tools-checklists/randomised-controlled-trial-rct-checklist/)
