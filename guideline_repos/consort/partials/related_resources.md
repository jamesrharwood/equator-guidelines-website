Use TREND if you are writing a report of a nonrandomized evaluation of behavioral and public health interventions.

There are several extensions to CONSORT which should be used in addition to the generic guideline, these include:

* [CONSORT for abstracts](/reporting-guidelines/consort-abstracts/),
* [CONSORT harms](/reporting-guidelines/consort-harms/),
* [CONSORT outcomes](/reporting-guidelines/guidelines-for-reporting-outcomes-in-trial-reports-the-consort-outcomes-2022-extension/),
* [TIDieR](/reporting-guidelines/tidier/) for intervention description,
* [CONSORT for social and psychological interventions](/reporting-guidelines/consort-spi/),
* [CONSORT for non pharmocological interventions](/reporting-guidelines/consort-non-pharmacological/),
* [CONSORT for pragmatic trials](/reporting-guidelines/consort-pragmatic/).

For appraising the quality of a study's design or conduct, consider an appraisal tool like the [CASP Randomised Controlled Trial Checklist](https://casp-uk.net/casp-tools-checklists/randomised-controlled-trial-rct-checklist/)
